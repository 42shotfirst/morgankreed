\section{What We Need From bunq}

\begin{itemize}
\item bunq's core banking system exposes or will expose interfaces sufficient for the payment messaging layer. Architecture jointly designed.
\item bunq provides dedicated engineering counterparts for integration design and development.
\item USD domestic payments only. Cross-border, FX, and multi-currency excluded.
\item BSA/AML, OFAC, fraud, and KYC remain bunq's responsibility. CTO on Demand designs and documents integration points.
\item ISO~20022 documentation procured by CTO on Demand and billed to bunq at actual cost, no markup.
\end{itemize}
