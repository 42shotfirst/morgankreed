\section{Compliance \& Regulatory}

The technical integration and the regulatory framework are not separate workstreams. Every payment rail built in this engagement has compliance obligations attached to it. CTO on Demand carries both.

\subsection{Included: Compliance Documentation Audit}

Before anything is built, CTO on Demand conducts a full review of bunq's existing compliance documentation\dash every policy, procedure, framework, and control assessed against US regulatory standards (BSA/AML, FinCEN, OFAC, and network-specific requirements). Included at no additional charge.

Deliverables:
\begin{itemize}
\item Complete gap analysis across all existing documentation
\item Specific recommendations for what needs to be created, updated, or restructured
\item Prioritised action plan aligned to the conditional approval timeline
\item Clear picture of what bunq's CCO needs in place before the regulator's on-site examination
\end{itemize}

\subsection{One-Off Compliance Documents}

Each document built from CTO on Demand's library of 50+ US-specific templates, customised to bunq's business model, digital onboarding workflows, and cross-border payment structure. Fixed fee, scoped on request.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{BSA/AML Programme}\dash built to pass examination, not just satisfy a checklist
\item \textbf{KYC/KYB Procedures}\dash tailored to bunq's digital onboarding model
\item \textbf{Sanctions Screening Controls}\dash OFAC compliance framework
\item \textbf{Transaction Monitoring Framework}\dash rules-based and AI-assisted, aligned to FinCEN guidance
\item \textbf{Consumer Complaint Policy}\dash US-specific handling procedures
\item \textbf{Recordkeeping Procedures}\dash BSA, FinCEN, and state-level obligations
\item \textbf{Regulatory Reporting Framework}\dash CTR, SAR, and state-specific obligations
\item \textbf{Payment Rail Strategic Advisory}\dash RTP vs.\ FedNow analysis, sequencing strategy
\end{itemize}
